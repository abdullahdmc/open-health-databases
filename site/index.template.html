What the access labels mean
    {{LEGEND}}

Every resource here is free for research. "Free" means no fee to obtain the data. Commercial use, cloud compute or some sponsor datasets may have their own terms. Fee-based sources such as UK Biobank, CPRD, HCUP NIS and MarketScan are deliberately left out.
